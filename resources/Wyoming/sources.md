## Student AI Usage Continuum for Empowered Learning (p.7 of the guidance)
- type: instrument (scale, 4 levels)
- issuer: Wyoming Department of Education
- url: same PDF as above
- link_status: verified-pdf
- notes: **PRIMARY-CONFIRMED, read directly from rendered page 7.** Four levels exactly as previously tracker-reported:
  - **AI FREE** — work entirely without AI; any AI use is an integrity violation; honesty pledge may be required.
  - **AI ASSISTED** — AI for specified tasks (brainstorming, planning, feedback); no AI content in final submission; disclosure statement and links to AI chats submitted.
  - **AI ENHANCED** — AI used interactively throughout; human oversight and evaluation of all AI content required; student responsible for accuracy and fairness; detailed disclosure plus chat links.
  - **AI EMPOWERED** — full integration enabling previously impossible creations; student responsible for accuracy, fairness, and originality; detailed disclosure plus chat links.
- **AIAS lineage PRIMARY-CONFIRMED.** Attribution line on p.7: "As adapted from: Dr. Leon Furze, Dr. Mike Perkins, Dr. Jasper Roe FHEA, & Dr. Jason Mcvaugh" — the Perkins/Furze/Roe/MacVaugh AIAS lineage, directly supporting the project's Perkins et al. 2024 framing. (WDE misspells "MacVaugh" as "Mcvaugh"; the graphic contains typos "mush" and "fariness" — worth noting if quoting the graphic.)
- The references list cites Washington OSPI Human-Centered AI Guidance v1.0 (Jan 2024) as an adaptation source for definitions.

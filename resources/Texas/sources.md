## HB 149 (89R, 2025) — Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA)
- type: statute
- issuer: Texas Legislature, 89th Reg. Session; signed by Gov. Abbott
- date: signed June 22, 2025; effective Jan 1, 2026
- url: https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/HB00149F.htm
- url_pdf: https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/pdf/HB00149F.pdf
- bill_analysis: https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/analysis/html/HB00149S.htm
- landing_page: https://capitol.texas.gov/BillLookup/History.aspx?LegSess=89R&Bill=HB149
- link_status: verified-page
- notes: **IMPORTANT NUANCE ON THE CARVE-OUT.** The explicit exclusion in the enrolled text is that "governmental entity" (Sec. 552.001) does NOT include "an institution of higher education, as defined by Section 61.003, Education Code" (nor hospital districts). That is a **higher-ed** carve-out from the governmental-entity duties, not a blanket "K-12 education excluded" clause. The broader "education was deliberately excluded" claim (attributed to Sen. Royce West in VERIFICATION.md) is directionally supported by this higher-ed exclusion plus the absence of any K-12 classroom mandate, but the artifact should source the specific higher-ed exclusion rather than assert a general education carve-out. Verify against the bill analysis before making a strong claim.

## Senate Bill 1227 — Generative Artificial Intelligence in Education
- type: statute
- issuer: Idaho Legislature (Senate Education Committee); signed by Gov. Brad Little
- date: signed March 2026; effective July 1, 2026 (Session Law Chapter 71)
- url: https://legislature.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/sessioninfo/2026/legislation/S1227.pdf
- landing_page: https://legislature.idaho.gov/sessioninfo/2026/legislation/S1227/
- link_status: verified-pdf
- downloaded: [x]
- notes: Enacted text confirmed and read directly. Adds new Chapter 70, Title 33, Idaho Code ("Generative Artificial Intelligence in Education," §§33-7001 to 33-7008). Requires SDE (with State Board approval) to develop a statewide GenAI framework; each district/charter to adopt local policies; AI literacy standards, assessments, PD; procurement/vendor-disclosure; parental engagement; emergency clause, July 1, 2026 effective date.

## A9190 (2025-2026) — sub-ninth-grade classroom AI prohibition
- type: other (introduced bill, not enacted)
- issuer: NY State Assembly, sponsor Assemblymember R. Carroll
- date: introduced Nov 3, 2025; referred to Assembly Education Committee Jan 7, 2026
- url: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/A9190
- link_status: verified-page
- notes: Confirmed. Prohibits AI in classrooms prior to ninth grade, with carve-outs for diagnostics and explicit-instruction interventions for students with disabilities, and for staff administrative/planning use. Commissioner to issue guidance on permissible uses. Companion S10133 exists (not fetched).

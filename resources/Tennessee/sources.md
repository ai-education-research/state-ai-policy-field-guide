### What the enacted chapter actually says
- **Sec. 1(b)** — the UT board of trustees, the board of regents, and each state university local governing board **"shall adopt a policy"** implemented no later than **July 1, 2025**, and the Uniform Administrative Procedures Act **"does not apply."** The introduced SB1711 required UAPA rulemaking; any summary built on introduced text is wrong.
- **Sec. 1(d)** — boards must post the policy on the institution's website and submit it to the senate and house education committee chairs by July 1, 2025. Noncompliant boards appear before the joint government operations committee within 60 days after July 1, 2025.
- **Sec. 2** — local boards of education adopt a policy, implemented no later than the 2024-25 school year, with an annual compliance report to TDOE each July 1 starting 2024.
- **Sec. 3** — same requirement for public charter school governing bodies.
- The enacted AI definition is the "machine-based system... predictions, recommendations, or decisions" formulation, not the introduced "computer software... machine learning" one.
- Date caution: the BillInfo page lists March 19, 2024; the chapter signature page reads "Approved this 11th day of March 2024." **Prefer the chapter.**

- link_status: verified-page
- notes: **Correction needed in data.js.** The current entry describes it as restricting "AI tools used to assess or screen student mental health" with a private right of action. The BillInfo page shows it prohibits advertising or representing an AI system as capable of acting as a qualified mental health professional, enforced under the TN Consumer Protection Act at $5,000 per violation.

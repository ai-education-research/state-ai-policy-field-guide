### The critical finding
The document states verbatim that it is a **Spanish translation of Washington OSPI's "5 Step Scaffolding Scale for Students,"** from OSPI's "Implementing AI: A Practical Guide for the Classroom" (2024), used under Creative Commons. Levels as printed:

1. Nivel 1 — Sin Asistencia de IA
2. Nivel 2 — Asistencia con IA, Generación de ideas
3. Nivel 3 — IA Apoyando Redacción
4. Nivel 4 — IA Colaborativa Creación
5. Nivel 5 — IA como Cocreador

**This overturns the July 28 record for Puerto Rico.** The "nivel 1 tutor de apoyo básico … nivel 5 herramienta avanzada" wording in VERIFICATION.md does NOT appear in this document. No mention of "Conecta y CRECE" was found in the extracted text either.

**It also corroborates the Washington finding**: PR's five levels are a direct translation of Washington's five levels, which independently confirms Washington's scale IS a five-step student-permission scale. Puerto Rico's lineage runs through Washington OSPI, not directly through Perkins et al.

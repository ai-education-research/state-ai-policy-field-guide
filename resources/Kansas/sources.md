## KSDE statement of no AI guidance
- type: other (statement reported by a third party)
- issuer: statement attributed to KSDE, reported by the Center on Reinventing Public Education (CRPE)
- date: October 2023
- url: https://crpe.org/ai-disrupt-ed-13-states/
- link_status: verified-page
- notes: Exact quote verified: KSDE "has not issued any guidance to districts regarding AI technology and, as of right now, has not discussed any plans to do so." **This is CRPE's Oct 2023 reporting, not a KSDE-hosted statement** — date it accordingly, and note it is nearly three years old.

## Update — KSDE has since engaged (Feb 2026)
- The Kansas State Board of Education heard a presentation of the KU-authored "Framework for Responsible AI Integration in Pre-K-20 Education" (Basham, Vasquez, Fulchini Scruggs) at its Feb 10-11, 2026 meeting.
- Board materials PDF (live, 14.9MB, not text-verified): https://www.ksde.gov/docs/default-source/state-board/february-10-and-11-2026-kansas-state-board-of-education-board-meeting-materials.pdf
- **The framework is a University of Kansas product (aai.ku.edu/ai-education), not KSDE-issued guidance.** So the "no state guidance" claim still holds at the agency level, but Kansas is no longer inactive. The data.js entry (which leads with the Gaggle lawsuit) should note this.

## Executive Summary

Maine's AI Guidance Toolkit is an interactive resource organized around three sections — Leadership, Teaching & Learning, and Ethics — under the banner principle **"KEEP THE HUMAN IN AI"** ("AI enhances, rather than replaces our professional judgment," per sources.md). It carries two named mnemonic frameworks confirmed from the toolkit text: a leadership framework, **ROOTS** (Responsibility, Oversight, Opportunity, Transparency, Sustainability), and a decision framework, **HUMAN** (Halt, Utilize, Monitor, Authenticate, Note).

For a higher-education reader, Maine's value is in its portable mnemonics — ROOTS translates readily to a department or college leadership context, and HUMAN is a compact decision heuristic for AI use. Two cautions: first, the toolkit's full content (examples, prohibitions, any student-use scale) is not captured, so much cannot be verified; second, a previously reported "five ethical-integration principles" list **could not be confirmed** as a discrete list and should be softened or re-sourced (sources.md). Maine is reported to reject technology prohibitions, but the specific language was not captured here.

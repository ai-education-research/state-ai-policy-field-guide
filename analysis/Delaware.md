## Executive Summary

Delaware has issued statewide K-12 generative-AI guidance through its Department of Education and the Delaware Council on Educational Technology, announced August 7, 2024 (sources.md). The guidance is delivered as a set of web pages — reported to include "Understanding AI," "Integrating AI," "Classroom Management," and "Allowable Use Statements" — and uses the SAMR model to frame classroom integration (sources.md). Beyond that structural description, the substantive content was not captured in the provided files, so no specific principles, prohibitions, or instruments can be cited here.

For a higher-education reader, the actionable facts are three: Delaware has a state guidance effort (it is not an absence state), it is organized around SAMR and includes "Allowable Use Statements" (suggesting per-context permission language exists), and the detail needed to characterize what students were actually taught is not in the provided documents and would require capturing the live pages.

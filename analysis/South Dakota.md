## Executive Summary

South Dakota has no state education-agency K-12 AI guidance in the record. The state agency's silence is unusually complete, its homepage carries no AI content at all (sources.md). What fills the gap is not a state framework but a school-boards association model policy: ASBSD Model Policy IIBFA, "Artificial Intelligence," dated May 14, 2024, which the association's announcement says covers expected student and staff AI use. The policy text itself is not publicly retrievable, so its content cannot be verified here.

For a higher-education reader, the operative facts are two. First, South Dakota set no state AI-permission scale, prohibition list, or student-use vocabulary, so a graduate's classroom AI norms came from the district. Second, because many South Dakota districts likely lean on the ASBSD model policy, there may be more cross-district consistency than in a purely silent state, but that consistency is unverifiable from this record and depends on how many districts adopted the model and how faithfully.
